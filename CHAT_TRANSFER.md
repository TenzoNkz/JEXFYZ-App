- MOSFET gate drive: GPIO → 100 Ω series resistor → gate; gate-source 100 kΩ pulldown. Fan/Peltier are low-side N-channel MOSFET switching.
- NTC: nominal 10 kΩ thermistor, 10 kΩ fixed divider, Beta 3950, assumed 3.3 V divider reference. Active source uses exactly one calibrated `analogReadMilliVolts(PIN_NTC)` sample each second. User explicitly rejected median/trimmed/multi-sample NTC filtering after a prior approach; do not add it without renewed approval. Avoid blocking `delay()` or `while()` timing structures. Invalid NTC fails safe (Peltier OFF, Fan 100%, Adaptive OFF).
- 12V selector relays and 9V low-side selector are break-before-make: retain 200 ms deadtime, 1 second guard/settle, 5-second Peltier OFF→ON soft-start. During a rail transition, pause only an interrupted active soft-start to PWM0 before selector changes; preserve logical Peltier ON, then restart a fresh full 5-second ramp after settle. If ramp had completed, keep duty 100% through transition; if Peltier is already OFF, do not auto-enable. Preserve explicit OFF, safety and OTA output priority.

## 5. Current Firmware thermal, Adaptive and persistence contract

- Hotside limit configurable 40–50°C, default 45°C. Thermal safety follows the configured limit. Manual Peltier latch rearm requires Hotside ≤ `hotLimit - 1°C` for 5 continuous seconds; do not allow a single low sample to clear it.
- If Hotside reaches the configured limit, force Peltier OFF immediately and Fan 100%; documented hot-off stage constant is 30,000 ms. Recovery proceeds under explicit safety gates. Fan must remain 100% while cooling back into the safe range.
- Adaptive boot profile defaults to HOTSIDE. HOTSIDE_BATTERY profile is runtime-selectable and not persisted. Adaptive thermal safety runs a 30-second stage at 12V, 9V and 5V: Peltier OFF and Fan100. Five continuous stable seconds at/below `hotLimit - 1°C` cancels the stage; if not recovered, step down a rail. If still unsafe at 5V, terminal lock applies. Restoration climbs one rail at a time only after 60 continuous seconds at/below `hotLimit - 1°C`, capped by the saved Adaptive ceiling.
- Adaptive Fan PWM at 12V:
  - Downshift requires ten fresh one-second NTC samples; every sample and the rolling average must be ≤ `hotLimit - 2°C`; no meaningful warming trend; one continuous 10-second stable window. Then decrement 10 percentage points no more frequently than every 10 seconds, floor 50%.
  - Upshift requires four recent samples (one per second), newest-to-oldest net increase ≥0.25°C and at least two of three consecutive intervals strictly rising. Increase 10 percentage points and wait at least 3 seconds between upsteps. Reaching actual configured Hotside limit retains immediate forced 100% fan path.
  - Adaptive 5V/9V run full fan. At 12V the UI allows manual PWM only when policy allows (manual voltage, not Adaptive); supported range 50–100% in agreed steps.
- **Important unimplemented request:** an independent rapid Hotside rate-of-rise/slope cutoff that immediately turns Peltier OFF and requires manual restart/re-arm was requested historically, but is NOT IMPLEMENTED in the known V6.7 contract. Never describe this feature as implemented.
- Battery phone temperature is sent by App as `PHONE:BT=<Celsius>` at 1 Hz while the BLE session is ready; Firmware Battery TTL is 5 seconds, Battery thresholds currently 25°C for 5V and 35°C for 12V, hysteresis 0.5°C. This heartbeat is separate from NTC's one calibrated sample per second.
- Persistent preferences use a shared 10-second quiet-period debounce and only values differing from persisted shadow are written. Adaptive Profile is runtime-only. User specifically does not want a standalone Battery Protection Guard badge/status in the App; keep actual firmware protection intact and do not suppress a separately actionable localized safety Notify.
- GPIO numbers/function above are authoritative; a previous user correction stated Fan/Peltier GPIO labels had been swapped in older notes. Check live source if any later functional edit arises.
